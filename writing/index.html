    People were dancing in the showers: bringing lasting power to a Himalayan village

    In remote Nepal, village power systems often fail within a year. In Moharigaun, 10,000 feet up in the Himalayas, an international team built one that is still running nearly eight years later, and that brought the village its first hot showers.
